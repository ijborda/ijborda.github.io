## Intro

How AI learns something is very similar to how humans learn. At the start, AI is very dumb. It doesn't know anything and its predictions are pretty much random. However, some training happens and the dumb AI becomes a smart one. But how does the training happen exactly? The answer is backpropagation. Backpropagation is the heart of all AI. This is the way they learn.
